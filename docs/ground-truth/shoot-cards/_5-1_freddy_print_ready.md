# 📋 6 Quick Tips (read once before any take)

1. **Speak 20% slower than feels natural** — cozy uncle energy
2. **Never look at the camera unless the script says so** — looking past lens feels more cozy
3. **Always close your eyes for 1 full second after the first bite** — your visual signature, every video
4. **Don't smile too big** — small half-smiles, not laughs
5. **Take pauses where written** — silence is doing work
6. **The peel sound matters** — peel slowly, let the mic catch it
